# Acme Expense Claim Policy (sample, written for this project)

All amounts are in INR. Section IDs are used for citations. Keep the numbers here in sync with `limits.yaml`.

## 1.1 General principles
Expenses must be incurred for genuine business purposes and be reasonable in amount. Employees must submit claims within 60 days of the expense date. Claims must include the date, category, amount, currency, and a description that makes the business purpose clear. Vague descriptions may be sent back for clarification.

## 2.1 Meals
Meals are reimbursable when the employee is traveling for work, working late (after 9 PM), or attending an offsite. Meals on regular office days are not reimbursable. The limit is INR 1,500 per claim and INR 2,500 per day.

## 2.2 Alcohol
Alcohol is not reimbursable. The only exception is alcohol served at an approved client entertainment event, and it must not exceed 30% of the bill. Where alcohol cannot be separated from a bill, the claim needs manager review.

## 3.1 Local travel
Cabs, autos, and public transit are reimbursable for business travel within a city. Cabs are allowed for late-night travel (after 9 PM) or when public transit is unavailable. Commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable. The limit is INR 3,000 per claim and INR 5,000 per day.

## 3.2 Intercity travel
Train (up to 2nd AC) and economy flights are reimbursable for approved business trips. Business class requires prior written manager approval. The limit is INR 15,000 per claim.

## 4.1 Lodging
Hotel stays during approved business travel are reimbursable up to INR 6,000 per night. Stays above this limit require prior manager approval. Personal extensions of a trip are not reimbursable.

## 5.1 Software and subscriptions
Work-related software and subscriptions up to INR 5,000 are reimbursable without additional approval. Purchases above INR 5,000 need IT approval attached to the claim. The limit is INR 10,000 per claim. Personal-use subscriptions are not reimbursable.

## 6.1 Client entertainment
Meals or activities with clients are reimbursable if the claim names the client and states the business purpose. Team-only social events are not client entertainment; see 8.1. The limit is INR 5,000 per claim.

## 7.1 Receipts
A receipt is required for any expense above INR 500. Below INR 500, a written description is enough. Claims above the threshold without a receipt cannot be approved until one is provided or a manager signs off on the exception.

## 8.1 Non-reimbursable items
The following are never reimbursable: personal purchases, fines and penalties, gifts to employees, and team social events without a documented business purpose. Team social events may be funded through the HR team-events budget instead.

## 9.1 Office supplies
Small office supplies needed for work are reimbursable up to INR 2,000 per claim. Equipment above this amount should go through procurement.

## 10.1 Training
Courses, certifications, and conference fees directly related to the employee's role are reimbursable up to INR 20,000 per claim, with manager approval.
